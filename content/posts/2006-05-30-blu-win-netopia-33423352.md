I have recently moved to Switzerland and of course the USB (A)DSL modem I get is not supported on Linux.

After some investigations the ID 100d:3342 should be compatible with "Conexant AccessRunner USB modem". But this Conexant stuff works in 2 different ways depending on the firmware. Unlucky me, the existing Linux driver is not for the way SWISSCOM uses the modem.

After more investigations the only thing that has a chance to work is a driver from Olitec. After downloading it from http://www.olitec.com/pub/USBADSLV151-1008fr.tar.gz it appears completely outdated and does not compile with my 2.6.13 vanilla kernel.

How to go on:

1. USB analyser + trace analysis.
2. Fix the driver and hope it works after that.
