jfc'log is my logbook: a list of the things I have been hacking. I have kept it since June 2005, writing down the steps that got something working so I can find them again later. The notes are short and practical, usually with the exact commands and config I used, and often with the error messages in between.

The topics follow whatever I have been working on: Apache Tomcat, Apache httpd, mod_cluster and mod_heartmonitor, OpenSSL and post quantum cryptography, and a long run of Raspberry Pi and Fedora tinkering, from real-time clocks and wifi access points to driving a relay on a GPIO pin to open a street door.

## About me

Name: Jean-Frederic Clere.

I work on the Apache Tomcat and Apache httpd side of things and on mod_cluster. Most of the code I refer to in these notes lives in my own repositories.
